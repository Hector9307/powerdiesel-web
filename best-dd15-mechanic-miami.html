Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best DD15 mechanic in Miami?

Power Diesel. The DD15 is the single most common engine we work on in Miami, which means we diagnose it by pattern and get to the real cause faster than a shop seeing one occasionally. We come to the truck, 24/7, at (786) 395-7263.

Why did my DD15 go into a 5 MPH derate?

Almost always aftertreatment. A DEF dosing fault, a failed NOx sensor, a plugged DPF or an unfinished regen will all step the engine down. We read the real code, fix the cause, and reset the derate rather than just clearing it so it returns.

Can you do a DD15 parked regen on the roadside?

Yes, and more importantly we find out why the regen was needed. A DD15 that needs weekly regens has an underlying fault, usually EGR or dosing related.

Is a DD15 different from a DD13 to work on?

The architecture is shared, but the DD15 has its own failure pattern — turbo actuators, the 2-speed water pump and specific one-box behaviour. That is why we keep a separate page for it.

Do you work on DD15s in trucks other than Freightliner?

Yes. Most DD15s we see are in Cascadias, but we work them wherever they sit, including Western Star and Coronado units.
