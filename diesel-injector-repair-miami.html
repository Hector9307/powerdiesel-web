Frequently Asked Questions

Can you replace a diesel injector on-site in Miami?

Yes, in many cases. We first run cylinder and contribution tests to confirm which injector is bad, then replace it at your location when the truck and access allow.

My truck idles rough and misfires - what is it?

Common causes are a failing injector, low rail pressure, air in the fuel or a fuel supply issue. We diagnose the exact cause instead of guessing.

It's hard to start in the morning - can you help?

Yes. Hard-start faults are often fuel-related - injectors, a pump, air in the fuel or filters. We find and fix the cause on-site.
