Why is my MX-13 losing coolant with no visible leak?

That pattern on an MX-13 often points to the EGR cooler or an internal path rather than a hose. We pressure test and confirm the source before anything comes apart, because the repairs differ enormously in cost.

Can you clear an MX-13 derate at the roadside?

In most cases yes, once the cause is repaired. Derates on MX engines are typically EGR or aftertreatment driven, and we fix the fault rather than clearing the code and sending you off.

Do you work on MX-11 as well as MX-13?

Yes. Both engines share the same design language and most of the same diagnostic approach.
