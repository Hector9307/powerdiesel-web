Frequently Asked Questions

What makes you the best Detroit Diesel mechanic in Miami?

Volume and focus. Detroit DD13, DD15 and DD16 engines are a large part of what we see every week in Miami, so we know their aftertreatment and electrical faults by pattern, and we carry the tooling to diagnose them at the truck instead of in a shop bay.

Can you fix a Detroit one-box problem on-site?

Very often, yes. Dosing valves, DEF lines, NOx and temperature sensors, and delta-P plumbing are all serviceable on the roadside. If the box itself has to come off, we tell you honestly and plan it.

Do you still work on Series 60 engines?

Yes. Plenty of Series 60 trucks are still earning in South Florida and we service them, including the pre-emissions ones that newer shops no longer want to touch.

Can you program or reset Detroit modules?

We diagnose ACM, MCM and CPC faults and handle the resets and parameter work that on-site repair requires. Anything needing OEM-level reflashing we tell you up front.

Do you cover Broward as well as Miami?

Yes. Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Davie and Pompano Beach are all regular territory, typically 60 to 120 minutes out.
