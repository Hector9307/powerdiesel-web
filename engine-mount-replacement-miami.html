Engine & Transmission Mount Replacement — We Come to You

Engine mounts are rubber and steel holding a very heavy, very hard-working engine still. In South Florida heat that rubber ages fast, and a diesel leak soaks and softens it faster still. Once a mount collapses or the bracket cracks, the engine rocks on every gear change — and that movement is what shears the remaining mounts, cracks exhaust flex pipes, chafes harnesses and hoses, and throws the driveline angle out. Power Diesel finds which mounts have actually failed and replaces them at your truck.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know my engine mounts are bad?

The usual signs are a heavy shudder at idle, a clunk when you engage a gear or take off, the engine visibly lifting when you rev it, and new rattles or exhaust contact. We check the mounts under load rather than just looking at them.

Can you replace engine mounts on-site?

Yes. Mount replacement on trucks is normally done at your yard or location in Miami and South Florida — we support the engine properly and change the mounts there. Call (786) 395-7263.

What happens if I keep driving on a broken mount?

The movement transfers to everything else. Broken mounts take out the remaining mounts, exhaust flex pipes, radiator hoses, wiring harnesses and driveline u-joints, so a cheap job becomes an expensive one.

Do you replace transmission mounts too?

Yes. Transmission and gearbox mounts fail the same way and are often the real cause of a driveline vibration blamed on the engine.
