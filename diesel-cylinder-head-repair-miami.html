Frequently Asked Questions

What does a compression test tell you?

A compression test checks each cylinder's sealing power. A weak or "critical" cylinder points to a leaking valve, worn rings or a cylinder head problem — and tells us exactly where to focus the repair instead of guessing.

Do I always need a full cylinder head repair?

Not always. Sometimes a valve recalibration or adjustment restores compression. Other times, especially with worn valves, seats or a cracked head, a complete cylinder head repair is the only lasting fix. We test first and tell you honestly which one you need.

Do you work on Detroit DD13, DD15 and DD16 engines?

Yes — we specialize in heavy-duty Detroit DD13, DD15 and DD16 engines, along with Cummins, PACCAR, Volvo, Mack, Cat and other diesel platforms.

Is there a warranty on cylinder head repairs?

Yes — our cylinder head repairs are backed by a 1 year warranty. Ask us for the full details when we quote your job.
