Frequently Asked Questions

Can I keep driving with the check engine light on?

An amber light with normal power usually gives you some time, but many faults start a derate countdown. A red stop-engine light, low oil pressure or high coolant temperature means stop now. Call us and we come to you.

Do you just clear the code?

No. Clearing a code without fixing the cause brings it back and can trigger a derate. We diagnose the cause, repair it and then clear the fault properly with original programming.

Which trucks can you diagnose?

Cummins, Detroit, PACCAR (Kenworth/Peterbilt), Volvo/Mack, International/Navistar, CAT, Isuzu, Hino, plus diesel pickups (Powerstroke, Cummins, Duramax) and Sprinter vans.
