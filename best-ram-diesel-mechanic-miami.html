My Ram has low power and a check engine light. What is it usually?

On the 6.7 Cummins, sticking turbo vanes and EGR faults are two of the most common causes. We read the fault, check actuator movement and confirm before anyone buys a turbo.

Do you work on 4500 and 5500 chassis cabs with service bodies?

Yes. Upfitted work trucks are regular business for us, and we work around the body rather than telling you to take it off.

Can you diagnose transmission problems too, or only the engine?

Both. Shift complaints and engine complaints get blamed on each other constantly, so we look at them together rather than sending you somewhere else.

Do you come to job sites?

Yes, including early starts. Most of our Ram work happens in driveways, job sites and contractor yards rather than a shop.
