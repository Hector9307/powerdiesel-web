Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my truck say "SCR system fault, derate in 4 hours"?

That is an inducement countdown. The ECM detected a DEF quality, dosing or NOx efficiency fault. If it is not repaired, the truck drops to 5 mph. We diagnose the real cause and fix it on-site.

Can you replace a NOx sensor or DEF pump at my yard?

Yes. Most DEF pumps, dosing injectors, NOx sensors and EGR valves can be replaced on-site in Miami. We then run the aftertreatment reset and verify the fix with a road test.

My EGR cooler is leaking coolant — is that urgent?

Yes. Coolant entering the intake causes white smoke, DPF damage and in the worst case hydro-lock. Stop the truck and call us; we come to you.
