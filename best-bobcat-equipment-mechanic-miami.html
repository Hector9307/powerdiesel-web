Compact Machines, Concentrated Abuse

A Bobcat does in a day what a bigger machine spreads over a week. The compact diesel in the back is small, tightly packaged and works close to its limit, so cooling, airflow and clean fuel matter more than they would on a large engine. Meanwhile the hydraulics take the real beating: drive motors, the hydrostatic system, auxiliary circuits feeding hangers, breakers and augers, and the quick-attach hardware that everything hangs on. Most Bobcat calls we get are hydraulic or drive complaints, not engine complaints — and they are diagnosed very differently.

🛠 Drive and Hydrostatic

Pulling to one side, weak drive or no travel — diagnosed at the drive motors and hydrostatic system.

🔧 Auxiliary Circuits

Breakers, augers and hangers ask a lot of the aux hydraulics. We test flow and pressure properly.

🚧 Tight-Access Service

We work the machine where it sits, including sites too tight to get a trailer into.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best Bobcat mechanic in Miami?

Power Diesel works Bobcat skid steers, track loaders and mini excavators — engine, drive and hydraulics — at your job site across Miami-Dade and Broward. Call (786) 395-7263.

My skid steer pulls to one side. What causes that?

Usually a drive-side problem rather than an engine one: a weak or failing drive motor, a hydrostatic fault or a control issue. We test both sides and compare before anything is replaced.

My attachment runs slow. Is the machine underpowered?

More often the auxiliary hydraulic circuit is not delivering the flow or pressure the attachment needs. We measure it, which usually settles the question quickly.

Do you work on compact track loaders too?

Yes, including track, undercarriage and drive complaints alongside the engine and hydraulic work.

Can you reach a machine on a tight site?

Yes. Compact machines end up in places a trailer cannot reach, which is exactly why mobile service makes sense for them.
