Engine and Hydraulics, Not One or the Other

A machine that will not lift is not necessarily a machine with an engine problem. On CAT equipment the two systems are tied together: the engine drives the pumps, the pumps answer to the electronics, and a fault in one shows up as a symptom in the other. A truck mechanic who only reads engine codes will tell you the C9 is fine and hand the machine back still unable to work. We diagnose both sides — the C7, C9 or C15 under the hood and the implement hydraulics, pumps and controls that actually move the iron.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best Caterpillar equipment mechanic in Miami?

Power Diesel works CAT machine engines and hydraulics together — C7, C9 and C15 plus implement circuits — at your job site across Miami-Dade and Broward, 24/7. Call (786) 395-7263.

My excavator runs but will not lift. Is that the engine?

Often not. Weak or slow functions usually point at the hydraulic side — pump output, relief settings, a failing circuit or a control fault. We pressure test the hydraulics rather than assuming the engine is at fault.

Do you work on generator sets as well as machines?

Yes. CAT gensets share the same engine families and we service and repair them, including the starting and electrical faults that keep standby units from running when needed.

Can you service equipment at a job site or a pit?

Yes, and that is the point. Transporting a machine usually costs more than the repair, so we bring the tools and diagnostics to the site.

Is a CAT engine in a machine different from one in a truck?

Same engine families, very different duty cycle. A machine engine works at sustained load and idles far more, which changes what fails and how we diagnose it.
