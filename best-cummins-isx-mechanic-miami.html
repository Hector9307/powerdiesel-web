Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best Cummins ISX mechanic in Miami?

Power Diesel works ISX15 and ISX12 engines on-site across Miami-Dade and Broward, 24/7, including overhead adjustment, EGR work and derate diagnosis. Call (786) 395-7263.

Is an ISX the same as an X15?

No. The X15 replaced the ISX from around 2017 and changed the aftertreatment and control strategy. They look similar and are diagnosed differently, which is why we keep separate pages for them.

My ISX is losing coolant with no puddle. What is it?

A cracked EGR cooler is the classic cause on these engines. We pressure test and confirm the path rather than assuming, because the alternatives are much more expensive repairs.

How often does an ISX need an overhead adjustment?

Follow the interval in your service manual for your rating and duty cycle. Skipping it is one of the most expensive economies available on this engine, and we do the adjustment on-site.

Can you clear an ISX derate at the roadside?

Yes, once the cause is repaired. ISX derates are usually EGR, aftertreatment or sensor related. We fix the fault rather than clearing the code and sending you off.
