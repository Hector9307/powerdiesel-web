Big Power, Miami Heat, Heavy Loads

A D16 is usually doing the work nothing smaller would take — heavy haul, oversize, the loads that sit at full throttle for long stretches. In South Florida heat that combination finds every weakness in the cooling system and pushes the aftertreatment hard. The engine architecture shares a lot with the D13, so the diagnostic approach carries over, but the duty cycle does not: a marginal radiator, a tired fan clutch or a partially restricted charge air cooler shows up on a D16 long before it would on a regional truck. Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best Volvo D16 mechanic in Miami?

Power Diesel works D16 engines on-site across Miami-Dade and Broward, 24/7, with particular attention to cooling and aftertreatment under heavy-haul load. Call (786) 395-7263.

My D16 only overheats when loaded. Is that normal?

No. It usually means something marginal — a partially blocked radiator or charge air cooler, a tired fan clutch, or airflow restriction — that only shows up under real load. We test it loaded rather than at idle.

Is a D16 just a bigger D13?

They share a great deal of architecture, so experience carries over, but the D16 lives at higher loads and temperatures and its failures show up differently. That duty cycle is what we diagnose against.

Do you do overhead adjustments on a D16?

Yes, on-site, on the interval your service manual specifies for the rating and duty cycle.
