Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best Power Stroke mechanic in Miami?

Power Diesel works every Power Stroke generation from the 7.3L to the current 6.7L, diagnoses by generation, and comes to your truck 24/7 across Miami-Dade and Broward. Call (786) 395-7263.

Is a 6.0 Power Stroke worth fixing?

Often yes. Most 6.0 problems trace to a handful of known items, and once those are properly addressed the engine is reliable. What matters is fixing the cause rather than replacing one part at a time. We will tell you honestly whether your truck is worth the spend.

My 6.0 is blowing coolant. Is that always head gaskets?

Not always. A clogged oil cooler or a failed EGR cooler will push coolant and mimic a head gasket. We test before condemning heads, because the difference in cost is enormous.

Do you work on 7.3L Power Strokes still?

Yes. Plenty are still working in South Florida and we service them, including the older sensor and fuel-system issues that newer shops are less familiar with.

Can you diagnose a no-start at my house?

Yes. Hard-start and no-start diagnosis on Power Stroke engines is routine mobile work for us, in driveways and yards across Miami-Dade and Broward.
