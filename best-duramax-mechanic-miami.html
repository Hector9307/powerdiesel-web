Generation Knowledge Is the Whole Game on a Duramax

An LB7 with a miss is a very different conversation from an LML with the same miss. The early LB7 is known for injectors failing under the valve covers; the LLY years are remembered for running hot while towing; the LMM brought the DPF and everything that comes with it; the LML has a well-documented high-pressure fuel pump story that can take the whole fuel system with it. A mechanic who does not know which year is in front of him will test the same three things on all of them. We start from the generation, then test.

How do I know if my Duramax injectors are bad?

Rough idle, a miss under load, hard starting, excessive white smoke and fuel in the oil are the usual signs. We read injector balance rates and run a return flow test rather than replacing a full set on suspicion.

Is the LML fuel pump failure as bad as people say?

When that pump fails it can send metal through the fuel system, which is why the repair often involves more than the pump alone. We test and inspect before quoting so you know up front whether you are looking at a pump or a full fuel system.

Do you work on the Allison transmission too?

Yes. We diagnose Allison shift complaints, limp mode and pressure faults alongside the engine, since the two often get blamed for each other.
