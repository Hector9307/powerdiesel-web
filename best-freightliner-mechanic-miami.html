My Cascadia will not crank but the batteries are good. What is it?

On a Freightliner that is very often a SAM cab or chassis fault, a bad ground, or a starter relay circuit rather than the starter itself. We test the circuit before anyone buys a starter.

Can you work on the truck at the Port of Miami?

Yes. The Port of Miami, Port Everglades and MIA cargo areas are regular call locations for us, subject to the usual access rules.

Both. Multiplexed lighting faults, phantom dash warnings, dead accessories and corroded grounds are some of our most common Freightliner jobs.
