Frequently Asked Questions

My Sprinter says "10 starts remaining". What does it mean?

The van detected an AdBlue (DEF) or NOx/SCR fault and will refuse to start when the counter reaches zero. Call us before that — we diagnose and repair the cause on-site and reset the counter.

Can you fix a Sprinter in limp mode at my location?

Yes. Limp mode is usually a turbo actuator, boost leak, EGR or sensor fault. We test it at the van and repair it there whenever possible.

Do you work on Ford Transit and ProMaster diesels too?

Yes. Transit 3.2L diesel and ProMaster diesel vans are welcome, as well as Sprinter-based RVs and shuttle buses.
