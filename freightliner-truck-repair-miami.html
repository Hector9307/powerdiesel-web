Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work on Freightliner Cascadia with DD15?

Yes. DD13 and DD15 Cascadias are the trucks we see most. Aftertreatment derates, DEF faults, EGR, turbo actuators and electrical — diagnosed and repaired on-site in Miami.

My Freightliner M2 has electrical problems — lights, gauges, no-start. Can you fix it?

Yes. M2 electrical issues often trace to the SAM modules, grounds or corroded connectors. We test the circuit and repair it at your location.

Can you clear a Freightliner derate on the road?

We can get you out of the derate once the cause is repaired — a sensor, DEF pump or EGR fault. We do it with original programming so the truck stays compliant.
