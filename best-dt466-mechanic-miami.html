A Great Engine That Fewer Shops Still Understand

The DT466 is one of the most durable medium-duty engines ever built, which is exactly the problem: many of the ones still working in South Florida are older than the diagnostic habits of the shops around them. The electronic versions run HEUI injection — the injectors are fired using high-pressure engine oil — so a hard start, a miss or a no-start is very often an oil-side problem: the high-pressure pump, the IPR valve, the ICP sensor or a leak in the high-pressure oil system. Chasing that with a fuel-system mindset gets you nowhere. We know the difference.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best DT466 mechanic in Miami?

Power Diesel still works both mechanical and electronic DT466 engines, including HEUI diagnosis, on-site across Miami-Dade and Broward. Call (786) 395-7263.

My DT466 cranks but will not start. Where do you look first?

On an electronic DT466 that is usually the high-pressure oil side — the IPR valve, the ICP sensor, the high-pressure pump or a leak in the system — because the injectors need oil pressure to fire. We measure ICP before touching the fuel system.

Are DT466 parts still available?

Yes, for the common wear and failure items. Availability is a real consideration on some components, so we confirm what a repair needs before starting rather than leaving your truck apart.

Do you service school buses and shuttle fleets?

Yes. Bus yards and shuttle fleets are a large share of the DT466 work we do, both scheduled maintenance and breakdowns.

Is the DT466 worth keeping in an older truck?

Usually yes. The engine typically outlasts the body and chassis around it, so the honest question is the condition of the rest of the truck. We will tell you what we see.
