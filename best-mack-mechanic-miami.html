Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best Mack mechanic in Miami?

Power Diesel works Mack MP7, MP8 and MP10 engines on-site across Miami-Dade and Broward, 24/7, and we understand vocational duty cycles as well as highway ones. Call (786) 395-7263.

My Granite keeps clogging its DPF. Is that normal?

It is common on short-trip, high-idle work but it is not something you have to live with. We look at regen history, EGR condition and how the truck is actually being run, then fix the cause rather than just burning the filter off again.

Can you diagnose mDRIVE problems?

Yes. Many mDRIVE complaints come down to clutch actuator wear, sensor faults or adjustment, not a failed transmission. We test before anyone talks about a replacement.

Do you come out to construction sites?

Yes, including early starts. Job site, quarry, yard or roadside — we service Macks wherever they stop.

Are Mack and Volvo repairs the same to you?

They share driveline and engine architecture, so the experience carries across, but we treat them as separate specialties because the fault patterns and vocational use differ.
