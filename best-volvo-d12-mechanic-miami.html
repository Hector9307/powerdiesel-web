Old Iron, Still Earning

The D12 comes from before the emissions hardware took over the engine bay, which makes it simpler to diagnose and, honestly, cheaper to keep running — if the mechanic knows it. These engines use electronically controlled unit injectors driven off the camshaft rather than a common rail, so a miss or a hard start is diagnosed completely differently from a modern engine. There is no DPF or SCR to chase. What there is: injector and cam follower wear, overhead adjustment, cooling system condition, and the electrical aging you would expect from a truck that has spent twenty years in salt air. We work all of it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who still works on Volvo D12 engines in Miami?

Power Diesel does. We service pre-emissions Volvo D12 and VED12 engines on-site across Miami-Dade and Broward, including the overhead and injector work these engines need. Call (786) 395-7263.

How is a D12 different to diagnose from a D13?

The D12 uses electronically controlled unit injectors driven off the camshaft, not a common rail, and it has no DPF or SCR to complicate the picture. The approach is genuinely different, which is why it has its own page.

Are D12 parts still available?

For the common wear items, yes. Some components take longer to source, so we confirm availability before we start rather than leaving your truck apart waiting.

Is it worth keeping a D12 truck running?

Often yes, precisely because there is no emissions hardware to fail. The honest question is the condition of the chassis and driveline around the engine, and we will tell you what we find.

Do you do overhead adjustments on a D12?

Yes, on-site. On these engines it directly affects starting, smoke and power, and it is routine work for us.
