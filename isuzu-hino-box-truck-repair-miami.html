Frequently Asked Questions

My Isuzu NPR is in limp mode with a DPF light — can you fix it on-site?

Usually yes. We run the diagnostics, check the DPF pressure and temperature sensors, complete the regen if the filter is not overloaded, and repair the cause. If the filter needs cleaning we handle that too.

Do you repair Hino 268 trucks?

Yes. Hino 258/268/338 with the J08 engine — DPR faults, EGR, injectors, exhaust brake and electrical — are common jobs for us in Miami.

Can you come to my warehouse before the morning route?

Yes. We work 24/7, so we can repair or service the truck at night or early morning at your yard so it is ready for the route.
