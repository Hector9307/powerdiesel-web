Frequently Asked Questions

Do you do in-frame rebuilds?

Yes. We handle in-frame and out-of-frame rebuilds — liners, pistons, bearings, cylinder-head work and more — on trucks and heavy equipment in Miami and South Florida. Call (786) 395-7263.

My engine is overheating and losing power — what could it be?

It's often a cooling-system fault (thermostat, water pump, EGR cooler) or a head-gasket / oil-cooler problem. We pressure-test and scan to find the real cause before recommending a repair.

Can you repair my engine on-site?

Many repairs — gaskets, coolers, sensors, turbo and injector work — we do right at your truck or yard. Bigger rebuilds we plan with you first, with the time and cost up front.
