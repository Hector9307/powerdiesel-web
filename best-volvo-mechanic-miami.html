Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Power Diesel the best Volvo mechanic in Miami?

We diagnose Volvos with live data instead of guessing, we come to the truck, and we are available 24/7 across Miami-Dade and Broward. Call (786) 395-7263 and we will talk through the symptom before we quote anything.

Do you work on I-Shift transmissions?

Yes. Clutch actuator faults, gear position sensor issues and shift-quality complaints are regular work. Many I-Shift problems are electrical or adjustment related rather than mechanical.

My Volvo D13 keeps losing DEF pressure. Can you fix it on-site?

Usually yes. DEF pump, lines, heater circuits and dosing valves are all serviceable at the truck, and we test the system pressure rather than replacing the pump on a hunch.

Is Volvo different from Mack to work on?

They share a lot of driveline and engine architecture, but the electrical layout, the body modules and the fault behaviour differ enough that we keep a separate page for each.

Can you handle Volvo derates and limp mode?

Yes. We read the real fault, repair the cause, and clear the derate properly so it does not simply return on the next start.
