PowerTech Engines and Agricultural Duty

John Deere's PowerTech family runs across an enormous range of machines, from compact utility tractors to construction loaders, and the emissions-era versions brought the same aftertreatment complexity that hit on-highway engines — with a twist. Agricultural and nursery work means long hours at part load, heavy idling and dusty air, which is exactly the duty cycle that soots up an emissions system and clogs filtration. Add the hydraulics, the PTO and the implements hanging off the back, and diagnosing a Deere properly means looking well beyond the engine.

My Deere keeps clogging its emissions system. Why?

Agricultural and nursery duty means long hours at part load and heavy idling, so the aftertreatment rarely reaches the temperature it needs. We look at the underlying condition rather than just forcing another regeneration.
